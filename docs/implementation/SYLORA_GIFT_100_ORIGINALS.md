# SYLORA — 100 original gift templates (no TikTok clones)

**Goal:** gifts that feel native to SYLORA — AI Co-Host, Live Studio, creator craft, memory, soft Lumen atmosphere.  
**Avoid:** roses, rockets, lions, sports cars, yachts, generic castles, fireworks-only drops (TikTok clichés).

**Currency:** `price_minor` = SYLORA_CREDIT  
**Tiers:** Rare · Epic · Legendary · Mythic · Divine  

Each template includes: **slug**, **name**, **price**, **rarity**, **one-line effect**, **Co-Host hook** (what AI should do — never fake delivery).

## Why these beat TikTok clones

1. **Identity:** listening, craft, studio, co-host, honesty — not luxury flex.  
2. **System hooks:** many gifts map to real SYLORA systems (memory, avatar, status, dialogue).  
3. **Anti-cliché:** #88 literally destroys rose/rocket/car tropes.  
4. **Safety:** hooks say “never fake delivery” and avoid leaking private chat.
