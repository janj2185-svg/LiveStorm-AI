# SYLORA — Complete Product Design (Approval Gate)

**Status:** Development paused until you approve this entire package.  
**After approval:** Continuous full implementation begins immediately (see `POST_APPROVAL_CHARTER.md`) until production-ready — all platforms in parallel (Android, iOS, Web, Windows, macOS, Linux).  
**Owner checklist / major decisions:** `OWNER_SERVICES_AND_DECISIONS.md`  
**Visual language:** Light glass OS · champagne accents · Aura companion · island dock (phone) · cinema rail (desktop)

## Architecture locks (non-negotiable in this design)

1. **Gifts** — No gifts browse/send page in normal navigation.  
   - **Gift Shop** = purchase + inventory management only.  
   - **Send gifts** only inside: Live Streams, Video Streams, Guest Streams, Multi-host Conferences, Voice Rooms.

2. **Messages** = full communication hub: Voice / Video / Screen Share / AI Translation on calls / Group Voice / Group Video / Conference mode.

3. **Media stack (production-ready UI):** Camera · Microphone · Audio routing · OBS · Virtual Camera · Streaming settings · Recording settings.

4. **Music module (first-class):** Personal playlists · Favorites · Recently played · Creator BGM · AI playlists · Mood playlists · Royalty-free · Integrated player.

5. **Aura AI = heart of SYLORA:** Alive companion (memory, emotions, voice, facial avatar, personality, context) across live host/viewer chat, business, education, translation, planning, analytics, creative/coding/research, automation, content, voice control, realtime recommendations — not a chatbot shell.

## How to approve

Reply with one of:

- **APPROVE ALL** — implementation may continue against this design system and architecture.
- **APPROVE WITH CHANGES:** … — list exact changes; design will be revised before coding.
- **REJECT:** … — state what must change.

No feature implementation continues until explicit approval.
